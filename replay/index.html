PvpBot vs Theo

PvpBot is a neural network trained with PPO inside a real Minecraft server. It sees 284 numbers per step and picks a move, a look direction, an attack or item use every 0.1 seconds. Theo is the Carpet PvP mod's hand-coded fighting AI, set to its expert difficulty. Every frame below comes from a recorded fight, and every recorded fight is listed.

What the bot expects

The value network's estimate, at each moment, of the reward still to come for PvpBot. Above zero it expects to come out ahead; a win is worth +1 on top of the damage difference.

What each fighter held

How it learned

The policy copied a simple hand-written fighter first (imitation learning), because PPO from a random start never learned to land a hit. It then trained with PPO through 11 opponents of rising difficulty, while 9 more arenas ran self-play against itself and frozen copies of earlier versions.

What it actually does

Against sword-style Theo the bot wins mostly with the bow at close range, holding it about 90% of the time. That beats this opponent reliably; it is not all-round PvP skill. Its shield is almost never up.

Against bow-style Theo the recorded wins come from setting him on fire with lava. Fire damage earns no reward, but a knockout still does.

How these were recorded

Theo is spawned fresh for each batch and fights with the same kit as the bot. The bot samples its actions from its policy, as in training. Positions, look direction, items, health and projectiles were saved every 0.1 s (two game ticks) by record_fights.py.
